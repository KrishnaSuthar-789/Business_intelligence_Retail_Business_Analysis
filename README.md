# Business intelligence Retail_Business_Analysis
Creating a multi-page business report to answer the most important business questions.
## Project Overview
The **Retail Marketing & Revenue Intelligence Dashboard** is an interactive Power BI project designed to help business leaders, particularly Chief Marketing Officers (CMOs), monitor retail performance and make data-driven decisions.

- The project analyzes retail sales, customer behavior, product performance, marketing promotions, sales channels, geographic distribution, and order returns. It transforms raw transactional data into meaningful business insights through interactive visualizations, KPI tracking, and dedicated analytical report pages.
- The report is structured to provide a high-level executive overview while allowing users to explore specific areas of the business in greater detail.

## Business Objectives

The primary objectives of this project are to:

- Monitor key business performance indicators, including net sales, orders, average order value (AOV), customer count, and profit margin.
- Analyze revenue trends and identify changes in sales performance over time.
- Understand customer purchasing behavior and compare performance across customer segments.
- Evaluate product and category performance to identify key revenue contributors.
- Analyze sales across geographic regions and different sales channels.
- Assess promotion performance in relation to sales, discounts, and profitability.
- Monitor order statuses and returns to identify potential areas for operational improvement.

## Dashboard Pages

The report is organized into six analytical pages:

1. **Executive Overview** — Provides a high-level summary of business performance through key KPIs, revenue trends, and sales breakdowns by channel, customer segment, and region.
2. **Customer Intelligence** — Examines customer behavior, customer value, and revenue contribution across customer groups.
3. **Marketing & Promotions** — Analyzes promotional activity, discounts, and their relationship with sales and profitability.
4. **Product Performance** — Evaluates product-level sales performance and identifies important revenue contributors.
5. **Customer & Geography** — Explores customer distribution and business performance across geographic areas.
6. **Orders & Returns** — Monitors order statuses and return patterns to support operational analysis.

## Business Value

This project demonstrates how Power BI can be used to convert retail transaction data into an accessible business intelligence solution. By bringing together sales, customer, product, promotion, channel, and returns analysis, the report helps decision-makers investigate performance, identify areas that warrant further attention, and make more informed commercial decisions.

The dashboard is intended as a decision-support tool rather than simply a collection of charts. Its multi-page structure separates the executive overview from detailed analysis, helping users move from understanding overall performance to investigating the factors behind it.

## Project Scope & Limitations

The analysis is based on the fields available in the source dataset. Although promotion performance, sales, discounts, and profitability can be examined, reliable marketing ROI, return on ad spend (ROAS), and customer acquisition cost (CAC) cannot be established without appropriate marketing expenditure, attribution, and acquisition data.

Similarly, customer retention and new-versus-returning customer metrics require clearly defined customer-level purchase history and reporting-period logic.

## Project Goal

The goal of this project is to demonstrate practical Power BI skills, analytical thinking, and the ability to design a structured, business-focused report that supports executive decision-making.
